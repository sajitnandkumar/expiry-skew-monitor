First time here? One-time setup (2 minutes)

You need an Angel One trading account and a phone with Google Authenticator (or any authenticator app).

  1. Go to smartapi.angelbroking.com/enable-totp
  2. Log in with your Angel One client code and PIN (an OTP may come to your phone/email)
  3. A QR code appears — scan it with Google Authenticator (tap “+” → “Scan a QR code”). Your phone will now always show a 6-digit Angel One code that refreshes every 30 seconds.

Then, whenever you want the dashboard: enter your client code, PIN, and the current 6-digit code from your authenticator app, and hit Connect Angel One.

Sessions last one trading day, so log in fresh each morning. If this page takes ~30 seconds to load first thing, it's just the server waking up.
